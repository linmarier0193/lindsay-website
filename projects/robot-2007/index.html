2.007 Robot

Robotics & mechanical design — A hands-on mechanical design project combining mechanisms, fabrication, testing, and iteration.

The Challenge

The Design

My design focuses on gettings as many of the mind blowers as possible. The design approach includes material with friction to pull the mind blower out of its holder, a way to actuate this material, and a way to go extend to reach higher levels of mind blowers.

The Process

Reflection

This was my first big fabrication project. I learned how to use mills, lathes, fasteners, bandsaws, etc. A full on machine shop. I felt so at home there and I knew I had to continue to learn and grow as an engineer. I made a lot of mistakes, and honestly, I was very prideful. There are a lot of things I would have changed about this design, and a lot more prototyping that I would have done. I was a sophomore here, so I was overwhelmed with a lot of things at the time. Questioning the direction I wanted to go with my degree, being pressured into grad school by my advisor, wondering if I should drop classes, things happening in my social life and those kinds of commitments as well. Overall, I do wish I put more effort into my design as a whole, but I did still learn a lot about myself as a person, and the types of classes I wanted to take to further my growth. This class was a lot of fluff and felt like it couldn't exactly bridge the gap between theory and practice very well, especially in the time frame that was required of us, but I did learn how valuable feedback and talking to shop staff is. I learned how to fabricate on my own and what it means to design with fabrication in mind.
