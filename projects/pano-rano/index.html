Pano Rano

Solar powered water distiller — Turning brackish water into sweet water.

The Challenge

This project was for MIT's D-Lab: Development class. The south of Madagascar is very dry and gets less than 400 mm of rainfall a year. Locals don't have enough drinking water, and the water they get comes from rivers, wells, or potentially contaimunated groundwater.

The Design

After literature review on solar distillers, we decided to go with a double sloped design that took advantage of the position of the sun at all hours as it moves from east to west. We decided to use galvanized steel for heat attraction purposes, as well as to use welding as a manufacturing technique since it was readily available in the field.

Project Presentation

Reflection

This was such a wonderful opportunity and trip. I learned so much about what it means to design products that are meaningful and contribute to the wellbeing of a community. I learned how to get along with people professionally and across culture and language. There is so much joy and talent in people all over the world that working on a project like this meant we were moving one step closer to creating an environment that fosters education, art, and more.
