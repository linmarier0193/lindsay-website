Event Planning

MIT Gala 2024

A student run fashion show. A magazine release. 2024. Watch the full show here

The Story

MIT Gala began as the event release for MIT's fashion magazine: Infinite Magazine. 2024 was the last year that that was the case. MIT Gala had grown so much that it took on an identity of its own. A place where fashion creatives came together to show off their work. A collaborative space for designers and models and stylists to put on a show.

My Involvement

My Role

This year I was appointed Director of Photography and Videogrpahy. I found photographers and videographers to capture moments of the show.

Details

I created a backdrop comprised of the magazine covers that year for a photowall as guests walk in (which was also the spread I helped design!)

Memory

MIT Gala graduated from a dorm event space to MIT's student center, making it more accessible to the MIT community at large. This was also the year MIT Gala partnered with Arts@MIT, bringing in institute resources for our club.
