Event Planning

MIT Gala 2025

The theme is ~ water ~. Watch the full show here

The Story

This year's MIT Gala once again graduated from the student center to the MIT Media Lab (kinda a big deal). Our connection with Arts@MIT got stronger, and they decided we should host the gala in a bigger, more professional space. We added features such as a dance performance to start the runway, a jazz ensemble for our gallery precession, and MIT Live for the after show reception.

I love MIT Gala

My role

This year, I am Director of Set Design! I love this role since I chose the theme of water, and then made the runway a wave of iridencent fabric! Gloria made the projections to react to our runway music, which made the lighting so good.

Dance

Adding a dance performance really elevated our show. I asked some friends from MIT dance team Ridonkulus to perform and choreograph to the theme of water and they absolutely killed it.

Memory

This was our best MIT Gala yet. Layla and Pria got added as Directors of Fabircation and they helped keep the designers and models organized so I could focus on putting together the flow of the show. I love board so much.
