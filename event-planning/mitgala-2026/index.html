MIT Gala 2026

The theme is |reflection| this year. Watch the full show here

The Story

MIT Gala had been such a large part of my MIT career and genuinely one of the most rewarding events I have been able to plan. I love planning this event so much, and I am so glad to have been part of an organization that facilitates creatives coming together.

My last MIT Gala

My role

For my final year, I am Board Director! It happened in a last minute roundabout way, and I was still the acting Director of Set Design.

Theme

The theme of reflection was chosen at the beginning of the year by board. I chose a runway (a raised one this year!) lined with mylar, and changed the floor plan to match.

Memory

At the end of the show, I went up and walked the runway for the first and last time. I gave a speech thanking everyone who had brought us to where we are now, and hoped to show everyone how much MIT Gala has given me, and how much more it has to grow.
