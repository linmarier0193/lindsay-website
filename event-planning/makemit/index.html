The Story

This hardware hackathon stopped running in 2023, but my friend Tom decided to bring it back! I am a mechancial engineer so of course I wanted to help him out, and I had a lot of event planning experience at MIT, so I joined on as his second to handle all the logistics of making sure we had rooms booked, merch ordered, food for hackers, etc. This experience was so chaotic but so much fun. We were only a 9 person team with 400 participants to manage, but we did it!
