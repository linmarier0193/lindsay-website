About Me

Hi! My name is Lindsay (she/her) and I'm a mechanical engineer and event planner. I'm from Los Angeles, California, the daughter of Filipino immigrants, and one of four sisters. I graduated from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2026 with my Bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering (concentrating in Medical Product Design) and a Minor in Design. Throughout my time at MIT, I got to explore myself more freely than I'd ever been able to before. In this time, I deepened my love for fashion by running a fashion show, improved my maker skills by gaining access to lab spaces and makerspaces with 3D printers, laser cutters, mills, lathes, etc. I also learned what can happen when I work with wonderful teams, both technical and creative: I've become a leader, designed and fabricated many a cool project, made friends, and learned that feedback and criticism make the world go round. This website is a collage of all the work I have been able to do in the last four years. I am so proud of how far I've come, and I'm excited to put it all in one spot.
