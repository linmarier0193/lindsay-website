Design

Physics Fabricator

A manifestation of material properties.

The Story

Light has always been an interesting phenomenon to me, ever since I learned about its properties in physics. When the opportunity came to create a device that measured one, I chose it. It is difficult to "measure light" with some device, and I think I went about it in an interesting way: cyanotype.I am exploring how light reacts to obstacles - resulting in a shadow or diffraction - by casting light on photosensitive paper that changes color based on how the light hits it, I can track the way light appears to split or disappear.

The Details

Inspiration

Photographs are, in principle, a way of measuring light. This was the first thing that came to mind when I thought about light and its properties.

Why cyanotype?

I wanted immediate results on my measurements, and I was not able to spend a lot of money. It was also extremely more practical than a darkroom.

Properties

I ended up measuring three properties of light: intensity, diffraction, and shadow.
