The Story

Travelling is something really important for me that I am so lucky to have experienced. Growing up, my family couldn't afford to go on vacations or even get passports, so I stayed in LA up until the moment I got to go to college in Boston. While in college, I started working and earning for myself. I got to go to Cancun, Madagascar, Portugal, Denmark, Scotland, Croatia, and home to the Philippines. Going worldwide has shown me how much the world has to offer, and I truly am not done yet.
