Hobbies

Nail Art

glitter, rhinestones, cateye, blooming gel, pearls, chrome

The Story

I started doing my nails when I was pretty young. I would always go into my mom's closet, grab my stepstool, and reach up to the highest shelf to get her bag of nail polish. That's how it all started. Then, I learned about stickers and, on the rare occasion I was brought to the nail salon, I paid attention to the way the nail techs cleaned and prepped my nails. Now, I do my own nails (and friends' nails!) with gel polish. I love experimenting with new designs and new types of gel. This skill is perfect for having a custom set for every occasion and every outfit <3.

My Process

Inspiration

I love scrolling through pinterest or saving reels/posts on instagram.

Details

I LOVE adding sparkle to my sets. I always try to add a rhinestone here and there or some cateye. The light shifting and catching on the nail is just so magical.

Memory

Each set reminds me of a point in time or a specific event in my life. So great to look back on them.
